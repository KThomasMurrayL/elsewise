ELSEWISE

What if you…?

Elsewise makes short animated stories about impossible situations, told to you: what if you were the last person alive, could talk to animals, or woke up on the Moon.

Watch on Instagram, YouTube and TikTok (@elsewisestories).

Elsewise Publisher

Elsewise Publisher is the private tool the Elsewise team uses to post its own videos to its own TikTok account. After a video is finished, the creator opens a review page, sees which TikTok account it will post to, previews every part, edits the captions, chooses who can view the videos and which interactions are allowed, declares any commercial content, and only then presses Post. It is not offered to other users.
