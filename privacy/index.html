Privacy Policy

Elsewise Publisher · Last updated 15 October 2026

Data the app uses

  • The access and refresh tokens a platform issues when the account owner signs in.
  • Basic profile details (account ID, display name, username, avatar) to show which account a video will be posted to.
  • The posting settings the creator chooses and the status of each upload, so problems can be reported to the creator.

Where it is kept

Tokens and settings are stored only on the Elsewise owner's own computer, in files readable by that user alone. Nothing is stored on any other server, sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Viewers

The app collects no information about people who watch Elsewise videos.

Deleting data

Revoking the app's access on a platform makes its tokens useless. The owner can also delete the stored files at any time, which removes everything the app holds.

Contact

Send questions to Elsewise by direct message on Instagram at @elsewisestories.
