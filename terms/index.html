Elsewise Publisher · Last updated 15 October 2026

Elsewise makes animated "What if you…" stories. Elsewise Publisher is a private tool used only by Elsewise to post its own videos to its own accounts on TikTok, YouTube and Instagram. It is not offered to the public, and no one else can sign in to it.

What the app does

  • Uploads videos made by Elsewise to the Elsewise accounts that authorised it.
  • Shows the creator a review page before anything is posted: the account, a preview of each video, the caption, who can view it, interaction settings and content disclosure.
  • Posts nothing until the creator chooses these settings and presses Post, and posts nothing to any account that has not signed in and granted permission.

Content

All videos posted through the app are original Elsewise animations. Elsewise is responsible for them and follows each platform's community guidelines and terms, including TikTok's Music Usage Confirmation and Branded Content Policy.

Changes and ending use

Elsewise may change or stop the app at any time. The account owner can remove the app's access at any time from the platform's settings, which stops all posting straight away.

Contact

Send questions to Elsewise by direct message on Instagram at @elsewisestories.
